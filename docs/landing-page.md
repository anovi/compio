**Offline**
## Keep calculating, even offline

Compio works without an internet connection, so your notebooks and calculations remain available wherever you are.

Previously fetched exchange rates are available offline too. Compio fetches fresh rates when you're connected.

Install it on your device and use it like a regular app.
